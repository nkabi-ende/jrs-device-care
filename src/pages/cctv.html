05 / GOOD TO KNOW

CCTV installation FAQs.

Can you install cameras I have already purchased?

Yes, subject to compatibility, mounting, connectivity and equipment condition. Share the kit details for an installation-only quotation.

Do Wi-Fi cameras need cables?

Many Wi-Fi cameras still need a power connection. Battery models are an exception, and their recording behaviour and runtime vary. We assess the system before quoting.

Can I watch my cameras from my phone?

Compatible systems support mobile viewing after configuration, subject to internet connectivity and the camera vendor’s app. We help you set up access using your own account.

What happens during load shedding?

Recording and remote viewing depend on power to the cameras, recorder and relevant network equipment. We can discuss suitable backup options based on the equipment and required runtime.

What determines the installation price?

Camera count, hardware, cable lengths, mounting height, wall or roof access, storage requirements and travel all influence the quote.

Can you help with existing CCTV faults?

We can assess compatible cameras, recorders and connectivity problems and discuss repairs or upgrades. Availability and compatibility are confirmed before work.

How should businesses handle CCTV privacy?

Plan cameras for legitimate security purposes, avoid private areas, notify people as appropriate, restrict footage access and agree retention periods. Your organisation should assess its privacy obligations.

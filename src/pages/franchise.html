The container model

A container repair shop for Durban neighbourhoods.

The model uses a 6-metre workshop for township high streets, commuter routes and other suitable sites. Our existing repair business serves customers from Newlands West, Durban. Customers can walk in for an assessment, approve a quote and collect their device locally.

Repair & diagnostics

The core service covers cellphone screen, battery and charging faults, plus tablet, laptop and other electronics diagnostics. Operators can refer specialist work to a central bench when needed.

Retail & local business

Stock selected accessories and tested pre-owned devices as demand grows. Build repeat work with schools, shops and nearby businesses.

Operator training

Learn the repair workflow, from job cards and quoting to quality checks, customer handover and warranty support.

The proposed partnership

We fit the shop. You run it locally.

JRs Device Care would fund and retain the fitted container and core equipment under the pilot model, reducing the amount the operator needs upfront. The full set-up budget and commercial terms are worked out for each shortlisted site.

JRs Device Care would provide

  • Painted, branded container with a service counter and repair benches
  • Core tooling, launch training, operating procedures and support sourcing staff
  • Job-tracking setup, supplier access and technical escalation

The local operator would manage

  • A suitable site or local site partnership, with support through assessment
  • Day-to-day team management, customer service and local outreach
  • Site operating costs, opening stock and working capital

Repair academy pathway

Train on the bench.

The JRs Device Care Academy builds our internal pipeline for sales, point-of-sale (POS) and repair roles. Our existing pool includes around 30 technicians who have completed NQF Level 5 electronics device repair training. Franchise teams are planned to draw from this pipeline as outlets open, based on role fit and availability.

What makes a good Durban franchise site?

Look for steady pedestrian traffic, safe customer access, reliable utilities and enough room to place a six-metre container. Local shopping nodes, taxi routes and township high streets are worth assessing. Tell us your suburb and whether you have permission to use the site or are still looking.

What could one repair outlet sell?

Count people who walk past the proposed shopfront during trading hours. Use that count to test how many stop for a repair enquiry and how many enquiries turn into completed, paid jobs.

Assumptions for all three examples: 2% of passers make a repair enquiry, 50% of those enquiries convert to a completed paid job, the average repair ticket is R650 excluding VAT, and the branch trades 26 days a month. Daily jobs are averages over multiple days. These are test inputs. Sales are before costs and exclude accessories, devices and contract work.

South African context. Stats SA reported that 95.5% of KwaZulu-Natal households relied exclusively on cellphones for telephone access in 2025. South African township research documents an active electrical and electronics repair trade. Futuregrowth recorded about 72,000 visits across KwaMashu’s Bridge City Shopping Centre on its reopening day in 2023. That historical, centre-wide count has a different scale from a shopfront’s daily foot traffic.

Before choosing a site. Count passers at the exact frontage on ordinary weekdays and a Saturday. During a trial, log enquiries, approved quotes and completed paid jobs separately. Use the measured conversion rate, local repair prices, margins and staffing costs to build the site’s projection.

Common questions.

What does a phone repair franchise cost?

We’re designing the pilot around a fitted unit supplied by JRs Device Care. Your costs would depend on the location, site works, opening stock and the cash needed to operate during the opening months. Shortlisted applicants receive the proposed costs and fees in writing.

Which parts of Durban can I propose?

We welcome enquiries from operators and property partners across Durban and eThekwini, including Newlands, KwaMashu, Phoenix, Pinetown and Umlazi. Each proposal depends on demand, site suitability and approvals.

Do I need a site or repair experience?

You can enquire while looking for a site. Tell us about your proposed area and operating experience. You can apply with a technician partner or explain how you plan to staff the repair bench.

Can I offer academy training at my branch?

Operator onboarding is part of the proposed model. Public workshops are a later option based on local demand, space and instructor availability.

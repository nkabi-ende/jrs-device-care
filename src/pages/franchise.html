The container model

A neighbourhood repair shop, built to fit.

The pilot starts with a 6-metre workshop for township high streets, commuter routes and other neighbourhoods with a suitable site. Customers can walk in for an assessment, approve a quote and collect their device locally.

Repair & diagnostics

Phones, tablets, laptops and supported electronics. Handle everyday repairs at the branch and refer specialist work to a central bench when needed.

Retail & local business

Stock selected accessories and tested pre-owned devices as demand grows. Build repeat work with schools, shops and nearby businesses.

Operator training

Learn the repair workflow, from job cards and quoting to quality checks, customer handover and warranty support.

The proposed partnership

We fit the shop. You run it locally.

Genius Device K would fund and retain the fitted container and core equipment under the pilot model, reducing the amount the operator needs upfront. The full set-up budget and commercial terms are worked out for each shortlisted site.

Genius Device K would provide

  • Painted, branded container with a service counter and repair benches
  • Core tooling, launch training and operating procedures
  • Job-tracking setup, supplier access and technical escalation

The local operator would manage

  • A suitable site or local site partnership, with support through assessment
  • Day-to-day staffing, customer service and local outreach
  • Site operating costs, opening stock and working capital

From first enquiry to opening day.

  1. Introduce yourself

    Share your area, business or repair experience, and any site you have in mind.

  2. Assess the location

    Review foot traffic, site control, utilities, access, security, competing repair shops and municipal approvals.

  3. Review terms and launch

    Shortlisted operators review costs, working capital, formal disclosure and franchise terms before any commitment. Fit-out and training follow site approval and agreement.

Common questions.

What will it cost to get started?

We’re designing the pilot around a fitted unit supplied by Genius Device K. Your costs would depend on the location, site works, opening stock and the cash needed to operate during the opening months. Shortlisted applicants receive the proposed costs and fees in writing.

Do I need a site or repair experience?

You can enquire while looking for a site. Tell us about your proposed area and operating experience. You can apply with a technician partner or explain how you plan to staff the repair bench.

Can I offer academy training at my branch?

Operator onboarding is part of the proposed model. Public workshops are a later option based on local demand, space and instructor availability.

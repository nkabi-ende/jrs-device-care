A few helpful answers

Before you bring it in.

What devices can I enquire about?

Cellphones, tablets, laptops and other electronics. Include the make, model and fault so the team can confirm whether the device can be assessed.

How much does the academy cost?

The device repair course is R7,999. Request the syllabus and confirm duration, intake dates, equipment, payment terms and any additional costs before enrolling.

Can you support a whole business?

Yes, commercial enquiries are welcome from organisations with high device usage. Share your sector, number of devices, location and support requirements to discuss a suitable service arrangement.

Can I buy a second-hand device online?

Visit our online shop to browse available products. Stock, prices and checkout availability are provided by our Shopify store. For sales assistance, email sales@jrsdevicecare.co.za.

Should I back up my device before a repair?

Where possible, back up your files and remove sensitive information before handing in a device. Discuss access requirements and data handling with the repair team.

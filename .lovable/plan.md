

# JR's Device Care — High-Converting Repair Business Website

## Overview
A sleek, modern single-page website for JR's Device Care, a device repair business in Durban (Newlands West & Surrounds). Dark hero transitioning to lighter sections, with yellow/gold accents matching the brand. Every section funnels visitors toward WhatsApp contact.

---

## Sections (top to bottom)

### 1. Hero Section (Dark)
- Bold brand name "JR's Device Care" with gear/phone icon
- Tagline: "repairs | maintenance | diagnostics"
- Location: Durban, Newlands West & Surrounds
- Prominent **"Get a Quote on WhatsApp"** CTA button (links to WhatsApp with pre-filled message)
- Subtle animated background pattern (diagonal stripes inspired by flyer)

### 2. Services Overview (Lighter section)
- Grid of service cards with icons: Screen Repairs, Battery Replacement, Charging Faults, Diagnostics & Repairs
- Device types: Phones, Tablets, Laptops, and other electronics
- Each card has a "Book Now" button linking to the intake form section

### 3. Pricing Tiers (Dark section)
- Three pricing cards matching the flyer exactly:
  - **Essential Repairs (Entry-Level)** — Screens R400–R650, Battery R350–R550, Charging Faults from R300
  - **Core Repairs (Mid-Level)** — Screens R850–R1,400, Battery R550–R900, Diagnostics R450–R850
  - **Premium Devices & iPhone** — Standard Models & Pro/Max Series with respective pricing
- Footnote: *Pricing varies by specific model generation*
- CTA: "Get Exact Pricing on WhatsApp"

### 4. Quick Intake / Onboarding Form (Light section)
- Interactive multi-step form to capture leads:
  - **Step 1**: Name & Phone Number
  - **Step 2**: Device Type (Phone/Tablet/Laptop/Other) & Brand/Model
  - **Step 3**: Issue description (checkboxes: Cracked Screen, Battery Issues, Charging Problems, Water Damage, Software Issues, Other) + optional text
- On submit: Opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message summarizing the form details
- Trust badges: "Free Diagnostics Quote", "Same-Day Repairs Available"

### 5. Why Choose Us (Social proof section)
- Key selling points with icons: Fast Turnaround, Quality Parts, Affordable Pricing, All Devices Welcome
- Customer testimonials (placeholder/editable)

### 6. Contact / Footer (Dark)
- WhatsApp CTA button (floating + in footer)
- Phone: 071 686 5256
- Location: Newlands West & Surrounds, Durban
- Social: @jrsdevicecare
- Floating WhatsApp button visible on all scroll positions

## Key UX Features
- **Floating WhatsApp button** — always visible, bottom-right corner
- **Smooth scroll navigation** — sticky header with section links
- **Mobile-first responsive design** — optimized for phone visitors
- **Animated section transitions** — fade-in on scroll
- **All CTAs route to WhatsApp** with contextual pre-filled messages
